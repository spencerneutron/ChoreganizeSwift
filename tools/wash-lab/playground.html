A gentle light on a section card's edge from outside, honey (#ffd978) by day and lavender (#aeb4ff) by night, like a diffuse strip: brightest at the border, falling off smoothly, never into the card. The glow marks a finished section (each grouped card has its own); the wash marks the finished day; the action rows are controls and get neither. Toggling a chore eases the rim between levels. B: the border is retraced round the whole perimeter from the corner the wave enters, with a bright head, over the resting fraction, then settles. Toggling a chore off takes it back.
