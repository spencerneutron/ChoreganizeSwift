# Choreganize Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-JUNE-13_

Choreganize ("the app") is a chore-organizing app for iPhone and iPad. This
policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: **the app is
built so that your data stays in your own iCloud account and on your devices, and
the developer does not collect it or run any server that receives it.**

## The data the app works with

The app stores the content you create — chores, areas, completion history, locked
days, and household details — in two places:

- **On your device**, using Apple's Core Data framework.
- **In your iCloud account**, using Apple's CloudKit (via
  `NSPersistentCloudKitContainer`), so your data syncs across your own devices.

The app does not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts, and it contains
no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## What the developer can and can't access

The app does not send your content to any server operated by the developer —
there is no such server. Your data is stored in your own iCloud account and on
your devices, and is handled by Apple under
[Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
The developer does not have access to the contents of your private iCloud data
and does not receive your chores or related information.

Apple may make aggregate, non-identifying information about app usage available to
the developer (for example, CloudKit storage and sync metrics). This does not
include the contents of your data. The developer does not sell your information or
share it for advertising.

## Household sharing

If you choose to share a Household, the app uses Apple's CloudKit sharing. When
you invite someone, Apple's sharing system exchanges the limited information
needed to set up and manage the share — for example, the contact you use to
invite a participant (such as an email address or phone number) and basic iCloud
identity information for the people in the share. This information is handled by
Apple as part of iCloud sharing; it is not collected by or sent to the developer.
People you add to a Household can see and edit the chores in that shared
Household.

You can stop sharing, or remove yourself from a shared Household, from within the
app or your device's sharing settings.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a small, temporary log on your device to help with troubleshooting,
which you can view in the app. These logs stay on your device unless you choose to
share them (for example, when sending feedback). The app does not automatically
transmit logs or diagnostics to the developer.

## Notifications

The app uses Apple's push notification service to keep shared data up to date;
that delivery is handled by Apple.

You can also turn on **local reminders** for chores that still need attention.
These are scheduled and shown on your device based on the days and time you
choose, and which chores remain is computed on your device. Reminder settings and
content are not collected by or sent to the developer.

## Your control over your data

- You can add, edit, and delete your chores and related data at any time in the app.
- Deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can remove the app's
  iCloud data through your device's iCloud/storage settings.
- Because the data syncs through your iCloud account, the retention and deletion
  of that synced data are ultimately governed by Apple and your device/iCloud
  settings.

## Children

The app is general-audience and does not knowingly request or collect personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the
"Last updated" date above will change.
